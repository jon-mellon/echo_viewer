      Privacy, validation & limitations

      Private by default. Paper text is kept in this browser and sent to OpenRouter for automatic claim extraction after connection, then for qualification and review when you choose a claim. Publication is a separate action. Manually review the results, the original paper, its controls, and the supporting evidence before relying on or sharing them.

      Validation is not yet complete. Expert-review non-confirmation rate: x% (placeholder, not a measured result). Individual AI flags have not been independently verified and could be incorrect. Manually check the paper before treating a flag as an omission.

      The preview supports person-level nonrandomized observational claims with an explicit or implied causal interpretation, including unadjusted estimates. Full article text is required.

      Connection settings

      A key capped at $5 or less, with no reset, is required. The extension remembers it for this Chrome session by default, across panel closes and reloads. It is never included in paper exports.

      This optional mode stores the key unencrypted in this browser profile, accessible only to trusted extension contexts. Someone with access to your profile could recover it. Use Forget connection here and revoke the key in OpenRouter to end access.
